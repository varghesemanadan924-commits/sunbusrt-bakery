About Us

Baked from Scratch, Rooted in Community

Started in 2014 by two lifelong friends with a single deck oven and a passion for fermentation, Sunburst Bakery & Brew has grown into the neighborhood's favorite gathering spot.

We partner directly with regional grain farmers and local dairy producers to ensure every croissant, loaf, and latte bursts with real flavor.

🌾 Local Grains

Stone-milled flour sourced within 50 miles.

☕ Fair Trade Brew

Ethically sourced beans roasted in-house weekly.

💛 Zero Waste

Unsold loaves donated daily to local shelters.
